THE NOMU CREED

Beyond water. Beyond artificial sports drinks.

Traditional hydration drinks overload your body with synthetic food dyes, high fructose corn syrup, and harsh sodium. Nomu takes inspiration from Japanese botanical equilibrium.

A balanced ratio of 320mg Potassium, 180mg Himalayan pink salt, and 20mg ocean magnesium that absorbs rapidly at cellular level without stomach distress.

Principle II

Real whole-fruit Tokushima yuzu, Uji shade-grown sencha tea, and crushed shiso leaf. No "natural flavors" masking synthetic lab chemistry.
